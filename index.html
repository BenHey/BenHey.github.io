Benjamin Heymann

Criteo AI Lab

I am a Staff Research Scientist at Criteo AI Lab, where I am the lead of the Performance Science team. I am a member of the join Inria/Criteo FairPlay team. I have a focus on game theory. I dedicate time to both applied and theoretical topics.

I hold a degree in engineering from the Ecole polytechnique (Paris), a Master of Science in Operations Research from Columbia University (New York) and a PhD in applied mathematics from the Ecole polytechnique/Inria.

I am currently co-advising Mélissa Tamine.

I was honoured to receive a Siebel Scholarship in 2016.
